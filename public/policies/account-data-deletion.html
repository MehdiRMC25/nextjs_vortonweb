Account and Data Deletion Request — Vorton App

This page supports Google Play Data safety disclosures and user privacy requests.

If you would like to delete your Vorton account and associated personal data, please follow the steps below.

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email request to: support@vorton.uk
  2. Use the subject line: Account Deletion Request
  3. Include the following information:
    • Your registered email address or phone number
    • Your full name (if available)

Our team will process your request within 3–5 business days.

Data deletion details

  • Your account and personal data (for example name, contact details, and order history) will be permanently deleted where applicable.
  • Some transactional data (for example invoices or payment records) may be retained if required by law or regulatory obligations.

Questions

If you have any questions, please contact us at support@vorton.uk.
