# Reward Points

Our rewards programme is designed to reward loyal customers while preserving the quality and value of our collections.

## Earning Points

Earn points on every purchase (amounts in USD / $):

Up to $72 → 2% back  
$72–$180 → 3.5% back  
$180+ → 5% back

Points are calculated on the eligible purchase amount (excluding delivery, services, non-eligible items, and any discounted or promotional items).  
Points are earned on full-price items only.

Conversion: 1 USD reward value = 11 points

## Using Your Points

Points may be redeemed as a discount at checkout  
11 points = $1 discount value  
Points may be redeemed in full or in part  
Points are applied to the total purchase amount excluding delivery/shipping fees  
The maximum redemption value of points per order is limited to 50% of the eligible purchase amount

## Membership Benefits

Your membership level is based on your total spend:

Silver  
3% off full-price items  
Early access to new collections

Gold  
5% off full-price items  
Early access to collections and promotions

Platinum  
8% off full-price items  
Priority service  
Exclusive offers

Platinum+  
10% off full-price items  
Priority service  
Exclusive and personalised offers

## Important Notes

Points are earned on full-price items only and not on discounted or promotional items  
Points may be redeemed against the total purchase amount, including discounted or promotional items, excluding delivery/shipping fees  
Membership discounts do not apply to discounted or promotional items  
Points cannot be earned or redeemed on delivery/shipping fees or additional services  
Promotions, discounts, and points cannot be combined on the same item

## Programme Updates

The company reserves the right to modify or terminate the rewards programme at any time without prior notice, without prejudice to accrued points and entitlements.
